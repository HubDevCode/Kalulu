## Kalulu 2.5.4

### A new look

Kalulu takes the style of its mobile design and rebuilds it for the desktop. Every new surface follows the colors of the playing cover, like the rest of the window.

- **Sidebar**: a profile card with the round logo and the KALULU wordmark in Ruslan Display, the Discover, Your music and Library sections in bordered cards, What's new and Settings rows with an arrow, and a plan card at the foot with the open file, folder and URL buttons. A narrow window still shows the icons only.
- **Search**: the search bar takes the accent color, with Cancel beside it. The moods and genres of YouTube Music sit on a sheet, grouped by section, as colored tiles with a tilted album sleeve in the corner.
- **Album cards**: wherever an album shows (Home, search, artist pages) its cover comes with its record sliding out on the right.

### Vinyl player

Settings → Personalization → **Player cover** picks what the player page shows: the cover, as before, or a vinyl record with the cover as its label. The record turns at 33 rpm while the song plays and stops on pause; the tonearm lowers onto the record and lifts off it. Its grooves and headshell follow the accent color. With Reduce motion on, the record does not turn.

### Now playing panel

- Three lines of synced lyrics: the line before, the sung line with a soft glow of the accent behind it, and the next one, sliding smoothly from one line to the next.
- The lyrics no longer freeze: they stopped for good after the panel had been out of sight for a while, for example while the player page was open.
- The whole queue is listed with covers, titles and artists, and is the only part that scrolls. The details and the lyrics stay at the top and the visualizer stays at the bottom. On a short window the cover moves beside the details to leave the queue room.
- One gradient covers the whole panel, so there is no more seam where the colors changed.

### Player and full screen

- The **Full screen** button is now at the right end of the player bar, after the volume. It opens the player page alone over the whole screen; Esc or **Exit full screen** leaves it. The lyrics button of the player bar is gone: the cover opens the player page.
- Leaving full screen no longer cuts the window at the edges: Kalulu gives the window back its size once the window manager restores it.
- The player page is centered vertically.
- The lyrics fade gradually with their distance from the sung line, with opaque colors that draw evenly.
- A wide video thumbnail in the player bar is cropped to a square like the other covers.

### Home

The colored header of Home runs to the edges of the page.
