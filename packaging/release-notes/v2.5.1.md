## Kalulu 2.5.1

### New: warning when yt-dlp or ffmpeg is missing

The AppImage does not include `yt-dlp` and `ffmpeg`, which Kalulu needs to play YouTube, YouTube Music and most streams. Without them the app opened normally, but clicking a song did nothing.

Kalulu now checks for them at start. When one is missing, it shows:

- which programs are missing;
- the command that installs them on your distribution (`pacman`, `apt`, `dnf`, `zypper`), with a **Copy command** button. On Debian and Ubuntu, yt-dlp is installed with `pipx`, since the archive version is often too old for YouTube;
- a **Don't remind me again** option.

Local files still play without them. After the install, restart Kalulu.

The message is in Italian or English, following the system language.
